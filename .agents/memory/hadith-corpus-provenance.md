---
name: Hadith corpus provenance
description: Limits of the imported hadith dataset's narrator information.
---

Treat names supplied by the hadith corpus as source-reported identifiers only; do not call them a complete isnad or show generated narrator generation, reliability, or biographical data as authenticated facts.

**Why:** The imported dataset provides Arabic text and a narrator/reporter field, but does not provide an authoritative normalized chain or rijāl biographies.

**How to apply:** Any future narrator enrichment must use a separately verified authority and make its provenance visible. Until then, label related records as names reported by the source and keep profile metadata qualified.
